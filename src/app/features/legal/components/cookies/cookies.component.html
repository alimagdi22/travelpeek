Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2024

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you browse websites. They help remember configurations, analyze activity, and customize content to give you an optimal flight searching experience.

2. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser preferences. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the performance and availability of certain interactive features inside Peek.
